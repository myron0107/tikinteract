# Demo media

The eight JPG illustrations and thumbnails are copied from the user's supplied
`tikinteract-animations-template/assets` reference. The original template is
unchanged.

The WebM examples were recorded locally with Chrome canvas/MediaRecorder:
960 x 540, VP9, 24 fps, approximately five seconds, gentle camera movement over
the corresponding illustration, silent, with a visible DEMO label. No AI service,
API, credit or paid external service was used. They do not contain or derive
from user-uploaded photos. They are illustrative example videos, not generated
character animations or demonstrations of identity preservation.
