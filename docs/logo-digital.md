## Final prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand. Refine this approved round TikInteract symbol to feel slightly more digital and precise, still serious, minimalist and immediately recognizable as the same logo. Keep the near-circle burgundy open ring, red tap/LIVE dot in the upper-right gap, short signal arc and centered red right-pointing Play triangle. Make edges perfectly crisp and vector-like, subtly flatten the formerly pill-rounded ring ends into clean precise chamfered ends, add ONE narrow precise radial separation at the lower-left part of the ring for a restrained digital segmented appearance. Preserve circular silhouette and all proportions, triangle corners slightly sharper. Only subtle burgundy-to-red gradient with clean solid surfaces. No texture, no noise, no glows, no shadows, no bevels, no 3D, no circuitry, no gamepad, no extra decorative pieces. Single centered symbol on fully transparent background, square canvas with small even margins, no text.
